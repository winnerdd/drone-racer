Flying Drone Racer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Flying Drone Racer ("the game") is made by Steady Flow Studio, a one-person developer in Thailand. Contact: steadyflow.games@gmail.com

Short version: the game has no accounts, never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number or location, and this version sends nothing over the internet.

1. What stays on your phone

Your best times, medals, recorded ghost runs, settings (sensitivity, camera and so on) and play statistics (how many runs, how long you played, how many frames per second the game ran, and error messages if something went wrong) are saved on your phone only. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, they are gone (unless Android's own backup restores them).

2. The play report leaves your phone only when you send it

The game keeps a short text report of your play: game version, phone model, Android version, memory, graphics chip, screen size, the statistics above, your settings and any error messages. It is stored in a file inside the game's own folder on your phone.

The report goes nowhere by itself. Only if you press Stats > Share (or Copy report) and then choose an app (for example LINE) does the text leave the game, and it goes to the app and the person you choose. That app has its own privacy policy. The report contains no name, e-mail, contacts, location or advertising ID, and you can read it before you send it.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • No purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We do not sell data and we do not share it with anyone.

4. Google Play

If you installed the game from Google Play, Google may give us anonymous, combined crash and performance numbers (Android vitals) from phones that have chosen to share them with Google. We cannot tell who you are from them. Google's own policy applies to what it collects: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and similar laws you may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. In this version we hold nothing about you: everything is on your phone.

  • To delete everything the game has stored: Android Settings > Apps > Flying Drone Racer > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game. Pause > Settings > New tester also wipes it from inside the game.
  • A report you already shared is in the chat or e-mail you sent it to. Delete it there, or, if you sent it to us, e-mail steadyflow.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will delete it within 30 days and reply.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a later version adds ads, purchases or online features, this page will say what they collect before that version is released.

  4. Google Play: ถ้าคุณติดตั้งเกมจาก Google Play ทาง Google อาจให้ตัวเลข crash และประสิทธิภาพแบบรวมที่ไม่ระบุตัวตน (Android vitals) จากมือถือที่เลือกแชร์กับ Google เราระบุตัวคุณจากข้อมูลนั้นไม่ได้ ส่วนที่ Google เก็บเองเป็นไปตามนโยบายของ Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  6. สิทธิ์ตาม พ.ร.บ. คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล (PDPA) และวิธีลบข้อมูล: คุณขอดูหรือขอลบข้อมูลได้ เวอร์ชันนี้เราไม่ได้เก็บข้อมูลของคุณเลย ทุกอย่างอยู่ในมือถือคุณ
